# Research-page template

Create or update one topic page in `research/`, named for its subject. Replace placeholders; omit sections that do not help the actual review. The template is not a scientific finding.

```markdown
# Topic

[Research index](README.md)

Question: what the user asked or which claim is being evaluated.
Related practices: links to canonical cards, or none; considering a product does not establish use.

## Objective: outcome name

Reviewed on: YYYY-MM-DD (user's timezone).
Use context: population, product/formulation, route, amount/frequency, duration, and comparator where material; distinguish known reports from unknowns.
Conclusion: keep current setup | consider change | unresolved, with confidence and the condition that would change it.

### Basis and practical fit

- Claim versus the user's desired outcome.
- Relevant dated baseline and reported experience, linked to the canonical card or reactions entry; reconfirm material older state.
- What the evidence measured and establishes, its quality and relevance, counterevidence, and material remaining inference.
- Consequential premise check and any uncertainty that could change the action or confidence.
- Incremental benefit, overlap, compatibility, cost/currency, access, effort, and preference where relevant.

### Sources

Source titles, direct URLs, publication dates where available, access dates, and the claim each supports. For a synthesis, note search cutoff when available, quality, and applicability. Do not claim an exhaustive review unless one was performed.

### Revisit when

A concrete change in evidence, needs, setup, or access that could affect the conclusion. This is not a scheduled task.

## Prior conclusions and revisions

### YYYY-MM-DD: affected outcome and context

State what changed or was rechecked and why. If a conclusion changed, preserve its prior date, conclusion, relevant reasoning, sources, and context here, labeled superseded for the exact scope with a link to the current outcome section. For a correction, list affected dependent conclusions and whether each changed, remains justified independently, or needs reassessment. Preserve unrelated conclusions. Retain original statements when recording factual corrections; add a dated notice rather than silently rewriting history.
```

Repeat the objective section only for outcomes actually assessed. Use stable descriptive headings for links. An ingredient mechanism, personal anecdote, or benefit for one objective does not validate the whole product.

Research links on practice cards identify applicability after a replacement: **Applicable**, **Needs reassessment**, or **Historical**, with the relevant context and a brief reason. Keep actual use, confirmation dates, and dated frequency/amount unchanged unless a new user report establishes a change. Ordinary state updates do not create research pages.
