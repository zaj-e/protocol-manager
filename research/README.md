## What belongs here

Record the question, objective/outcome, relevant use context, current conclusion, reasoning, sources with access dates, practical fit, and conditions for reconsideration. Assess a product separately for each claimed benefit. A negative or uncertain conclusion is useful when it prevents repeated investigation.

Keep stable outcome headings so practice cards can link to the matching section. Give each objective its own review date and applicability; a page edit date must not refresh every review. Candidate products belong here until the user reports adoption. Reviewing evidence does not confirm actual use.

## Revisions and retrieval

Show the latest supported conclusion prominently. When evidence or a correction materially changes it, retain the prior conclusion, its relevant reasoning, sources, dates, and use context in a clearly labeled prior-revisions section on the same page. Mark the exact superseded scope and point to the current outcome section. Preserve conclusions about other objectives. An unchanged recheck can add a short dated note about what was checked without duplicating the whole page.

Before reusing research, read the actual applicable section and its correction or reassessment notices. Search topic names and claim text for related pages, including candidate-only reviews; do not rely exclusively on card links. Follow relevant dependencies without reading the entire archive by default. Reverify time-sensitive facts when they matter.

Personal observations and meaningful product changes stay with their canonical practice cards; reported adverse reactions live in [state/reactions.md](../state/reactions.md). Use links rather than duplicate histories. Research may cite a dated user report as context but does not become a second canonical copy of it.
